Part 1

Line List, Valve List & Piping Material Specification

  • Prepared and revised the project's line list and manual valve list — over 1,400 lines and 1,500 valves, tracked against successive P&ID revisions
  • Wrote the Piping Material Specification (PMS) covering pipe classes, ratings and end connections for each service
  • Sized lines from 1/2" up to 28", across PVC, PE (polyethylene), SS (stainless steel) and CS (carbon steel) piping
  • Identified critical lines downstream of pumps and heat exchangers and transmitted them for stress analysis

Part 2

Modeling, Stress Analysis & QC Documentation

  • 3D piping design in AVEVA PDMS, generating isometrics for the whole site
  • Stress analysis of critical lines in CAESAR II against ASME B31.3
  • Wrote the hydrotest specification for all PVC, PE, SS and CS lines
  • Prepared the Welding Procedure Specification (WPS) and the painting procedure, and controlled the 3‑layer painting thickness on site against spec
Part 3

Dosing Pump Skid — Design & QC

  • Designed the RO/UF backwash dosing pump skid — piping, structural frame and equipment layout — in SolidWorks
  • QC'd the skid's fabrication against the 3D model and piping isometrics before shipment
Part 4

Pipe Supports — Design, FEA & Fabrication

  • Generated piping support drawings for the whole site and tagged every support against the isometrics and equipment/skid layout
  • Ran finite element analysis on the pipe support structures — meshing and resultant displacement (URES) under piping and thermal loads
  • Followed supports through fabrication and painting, from raw welded frames to finished, coated units ready for install
